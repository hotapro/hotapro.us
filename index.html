FAQ

Common questions.

How does international collaboration work?

We work remotely. After an intro conversation we send a written proposal. Work proceeds in milestones you can review, with progress shared through agreed channels.

What do you need to start a project?

Your goals, target audience, existing assets (if any), examples you like, and any technical or legal constraints. We help shape the rest during discovery.

How will we communicate?

Mainly by email and scheduled video calls. Meeting times and response expectations are discussed and agreed per project; we do not make blanket promises about availability.

How is scope handled?

Scope, deliverables and change procedures are written in the proposal. Requests outside the agreed scope are discussed and quoted separately.

How does payment work?

Specific payment terms, currency and schedule are agreed in the contract. This website does not process payments.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Confidentiality terms, such as an NDA, can be discussed and included in the contract before sensitive materials are shared. Please avoid sending confidential data through the demo form below.
